# Community forum

[`/forum`]({{base_url}}/forum) is one place, not two. Every topic is either a
**Question** or a **Discussion**, and you choose which when you post.

| Kind | Use it for | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Question** | Something with a definite answer | "Why do my connectome weights go NaN after export?" |
| **Discussion** | Everything else — methods, papers, the field | "Is anyone fitting E/I balance to resting-state FC?" |

On a question, replies are voted on and you mark the one that solved your
problem, so the thread stays useful to the next person who hits the same wall.
A discussion simply stays open — nothing gets accepted, and anyone can weigh in
as often as the conversation needs.

![The forum](img/forum-index.webp)

Posts of both kinds share one list, one search box and one set of tags. The
filter dropdown narrows to **Questions** or **Discussions**, and — for questions
— to **Solved** or **Unsolved**.

**Reading needs no account.** Every post and reply is public, and search engines
index them.

## Post for the first time

1. [Create an account]({{base_url}}/web/signup) — name, email, password.
2. **Confirm your email address.** Until you do, the forum shows a banner
   offering to send you a verification email — click it, then open the link.
   Confirming grants the karma needed to post, and is what keeps spam off the
   forum.
3. Choose **New Post**, pick Question or Discussion, and write.

![Writing a post](img/forum-ask.webp)

Your **first post is reviewed by a moderator** before it appears. That is a spam
gate, not a judgement on your post — everything you post afterwards goes live
immediately.

![A question thread](img/forum-question.webp)

Every post carries a vote control. On a question, the asker also marks one reply
as accepted. Signed out you can still read the whole thread; the sidebar spells
out what an account would let you do.

Got the kind wrong? Edit the post and switch it — the choice is a property of
the topic, not something you are stuck with.

## Write a question people can answer

- Search first. The answer may already be here or in the [platform guide]({{base_url}}/docs).
- One question per post, with a title that states the actual problem.
- Say which model or experiment you started from, which backend, and which TVBO version.
- Paste the exact error rather than a paraphrase, in a code block.
- Say what you expected and what happened instead.

Tag your post — `schema`, `dynamics`, `connectome`, `backends`,
`experiment-builder`, `knowledge-graph`, `python-package`, `api` — so people
watching that topic find it.

## Karma, briefly

Karma is the forum's permission system: it grows as your posts are upvoted and
accepted, and unlocks more abilities as it does.

| You can… | Karma |
|---|---|
| Post, reply, comment, upvote | 3 — granted when you confirm your email |
| Post without moderation | 5 |
| Flag spam or abuse | 10 |
| Downvote | 50 |

Answering well is the fastest way up: an upvoted answer is worth 10, and an
accepted one 15.

## Moderation

Platform administrators moderate the forum and are notified by email when a post
is waiting for review. Found something that shouldn't be there? Flag it, and it
goes into the same queue.
