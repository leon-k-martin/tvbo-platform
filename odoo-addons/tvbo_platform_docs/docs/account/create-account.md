# Log in and create an account

The platform handles login and registration with your email and a password. There
is no separate identity provider to set up. You can browse and export without an
account; an account just gives you a place to keep your work.

## Log in

Go to [`/web/login`]({{base_url}}/web/login) and enter your **email** and
**password**. Forgot your password? Use **Reset password** on the same screen.

![The login screen](img/login.webp)

## Register

New here? Choose **Create Account** on the home page (or go to
[`/web/signup`]({{base_url}}/web/signup)) and fill in your name, email, and a
password.

![The registration form](img/register.webp)

After signing up you land on your dashboard at [`/my`]({{base_url}}/my). Its left menu leads to **My models**, **Shared with me**, **API keys** and **Your details**; the account menu under your name, top right, leads to the website, the documentation and **Log out**.

Next, [save a model or experiment to your account](save-to-account.md).
