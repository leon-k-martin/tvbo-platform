# The Knowledge Graph

The **Knowledge Graph (KG)** is the searchable catalogue of everything in the TVB
Ontology: neural‑mass and neural‑field **dynamics**, **networks** (connectomes),
**integrators**, **couplings**, **observation models**, **atlases**,
**graph generators**, and the **studies** that use them. It is open to everyone —
no account required.

Open it at [`/tvbo/kg`]({{base_url}}/tvbo/kg).

![The Knowledge Graph, card list view](img/kg-list-view.png)

## Two ways to look at it

The KG has two synchronized views; switch between them with the toggle in the
top‑right of the results, next to **Sort**.

- **[List view](list-view.md)** — a filterable grid of cards, one per entity. Best
  for searching, filtering by class, and reading details. *This is the default.*
- **[Graph view](graph-view.md)** — an interactive node‑link diagram showing how
  entities relate (a model belongs to a family, a study uses a network, …). Best
  for seeing context and discovering neighbours.

Both views share the **same search box and filters**, so anything you type or
filter applies to whichever view you are in.
